# Kapstan

**Kapstan installs the artificial intelligence that works inside your company,
under your rules.**

Applied AI, not a product you subscribe to. Someone learns how your company does
the thing today, writes the rule down, builds what executes it, and puts it in
production. What stays is yours: code, servers and accounts in your company's
name from day one.

São Paulo and Porto Alegre, Brazil · [kapstan.com.br](https://kapstan.com.br)
